### What's working · What's not
- ✅ Steady demand (~250/wk); churn improving (**11% → 7%** closure by cohort)
- ✅ **20%** quick second-budget — expansion signal
- ⚠️ **~55%** never funded; May cohort funding slipped (**52% → 44%** of actives)
- ⚠️ **May:** card attach **+233%** MoM, but unfunded actives **+18%** — issuance up, fund-then-spend loop weak

---

### Ask (next 4–6 weeks)
1. **Fund-first:** mandatory post-create path to first deposit (target April-level **~50%** funded-of-active)
2. **Spend loop:** post-fund **Issue card** → day 3/7 nudge until first txn (today **<3%** of budgets spend)
3. **Learn May:** replicate what drove **181** active cards in May without sacrificing funding

**Success metrics:** fund ≤7d · card issued ≤7d post-fund · first txn ≤14d · % funded balance spent on budget card
