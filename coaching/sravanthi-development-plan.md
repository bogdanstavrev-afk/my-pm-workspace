# Sravanthi — development plan (manager use)

**Purpose:** Raise the bar on product rhythm, OSC quality, stakeholder visibility, and eng partnership — framed as role growth, not reactive criticism.

**Primary public habit:** Post to **#ps-cards** at every **sprint start** and **sprint end** using the template in [`templates/ps-cards-sprint-pulse.md`](../templates/ps-cards-sprint-pulse.md).

## What “good” looks like in 30 days

| Area | Success signal |
|------|----------------|
| **#ps-cards rhythm** | Sprint start + sprint end posts every sprint; no chasing from you, Lora, or EM |

## Commitments (agree in 1:1)

### Non‑negotiables (her ownership)

1. **#ps-cards** — Sprint **start** and **end** posts (simple template); no exceptions when sprint boundaries move.
2. **OKR work** — Epic link + one line per epic (focus at start / outcome at end).
3. **Non-OKR work** — bugs, improvements, etc. as Jira links (including PM‑raised items — see open items below).
4. **Changes** — call out priority shifts at sprint start (“anything changed?”); same-day post if shift mid-sprint.
5. **Heads-up** — flag risks early at sprint start; forward-looking heads-up at end (no not-delivered list in channel).
6. **Dogfooding** — 2× 45 min/week on OSC; file or comment on anything user‑visible.
7. **Philip** — Joint planning each sprint; weekly 30 min bug/quality triage when OSC is in flight.

### Your support

- 1:1 every 2 weeks: review last two #ps-cards posts + one OKR at risk.
- Attend one cross‑team sync per month; debrief sync hygiene privately (no “someone complained”).
- Ask Lora to give **direct, in‑the‑moment** feedback when gaps appear (after you’ve set the #ps-cards expectation).
