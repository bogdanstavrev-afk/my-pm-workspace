# Skill: Weekly stakeholder update

## When to use
Whenever I ask for a weekly or fortnightly stakeholder update.

## Inputs needed
- @sprint-data.md (current sprint status)
- @decisions-log.md (key decisions this week)
- Audience: leadership, cross-functional, or team

## Steps
1. Read both input files completely
2. Identify what shipped, what's on track, what's blocked
3. For each blocker, identify the action being taken
4. Surface 1-2 decisions needed from leadership
5. Write under 300 words
6. Use bullet points, no paragraphs

## Tone
Confident, data-driven, no hedging. Lead with outcomes, not activities.

## Output format
**What shipped**
- [bullet]

**On track**
- [bullet]

**Blocked**
- [issue] - [action being taken]

**Decisions needed**
- [decision] - by [date]

## What NOT to do
- Don't list every ticket
- Don't use phrases like "I am pleased to report"
- Don't bury blockers under good news
