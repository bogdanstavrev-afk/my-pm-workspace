# Skill: Competitive Snapshot

## When to use
When asked to analyse a competitor.

## Inputs needed
- Competitor name
- Specific product area to focus on

## Steps
1. Research the competitor's offering in the specified area
2. Identify pricing, key features, and recent launches (last 90 days)
3. Compare to our offering in the same area
4. Identify gaps and opportunities

## Output format
**Competitor:** [name]
**Focus area:** [area]

**Their offering**
- Key features: [list]
- Pricing: [details]
- Recent moves: [last 90 days]

**Our position**
- Where we lead: [bullets]
- Where they lead: [bullets]
- Where we're at parity: [bullets]

**Strategic implications**
- 2-3 things to consider
- 1-2 immediate opportunities
