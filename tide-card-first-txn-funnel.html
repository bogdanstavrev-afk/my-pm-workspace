What stands out

Source: Tide KM and Card First-Transaction Analysis (BigQuery UK, run 16 Nov 2026). Scope: new KMs only. Virtual cards issued to or used by mature KMs are not included, so the Virtual figures are not representative of Virtual card activation overall. Unit = company (business_id). Cards = main cardholder, not additional or replacement, issued from 1 day before to 30 days after KM. First transaction = first CLEARED DEBIT CARD_TRANSACTION (cash withdrawals and refunds excluded). Rates are cumulative and use only cards old enough to observe each window (Table D of the source). Sep and Oct “no transaction” and “ever” figures are censored and not final; Oct 30-day rates cover only cards issued on or before 17 Oct. Known issue: from 25 Sep 2026 a bug stopped Online Debit issuance to about half of BCA intent, which lowers BCA Online Debit cards per KM in Sep (last ~6 days) and Oct; the rates describe only the cards that were issued. Auto-issuing of Online Debit is being cancelled from this month. Totals were not reconciled with Looker.
