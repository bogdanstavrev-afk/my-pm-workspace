You are writing a weekly update for leadership.

Read @sprint-data.md and @decisions-log.md.

Write a concise update (under 300 words) with:
- What shipped
- What's on track
- What's blocked (and what you're doing about it)
- Key decisions needed from leadership

Tone: confident, data-driven, no fluff. Use bullet points.

Output format:
**What shipped**
- [bullet]

**On track**
- [bullet]

**Blocked**
- [issue] - [action being taken]

**Decisions needed**
- [decision] - by [date]
