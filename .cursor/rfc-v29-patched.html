1. Summary and Goals

  • Summary:

This RFC describes the Cards service integration required to support Credit Flex Auto-Instalments - a feature that allows eligible members to set per-card rules that automatically convert qualifying card transactions into Credit Flex loans upon settlement.

The Cards domain must support storing and managing per-card Credit Flex configuration (on/off toggle and transaction amount threshold), evaluating auto-flex eligibility and initiation of loan after successful card transaction

  • Goals:

Enable per-card Credit Flex configuration (toggle + threshold) on member physical and virtual cards (excluding Team cards)

Implement settlement-time evaluation logic to determine whether a posted transaction qualifies for auto-flexing based on card config, member eligibility, threshold, and remaining credit line

Integrate with the Credit service APIs to receive dynamic eligibility status, credit line limits, interest rates, and to trigger loan creation on qualifying transactions

Handle eligibility state transitions gracefully - pausing auto-flex when a member becomes ineligible and restoring previous configuration when eligibility returns

Support the first-time activation flow including T&C/PG acceptance tracking before any card can be enabled

4. Proposed Solution

High level design

Configurration:

  1. credit_flex_card stores per-card activation , the user's chosen min amount, and the on/off toggle.

  2. GET /credit-flex-cards/eligibility - used by the Cards home banner ("Activate Credit Flex" entry)

  3. GET /credit-flex-cards?status=ELIGIBLE - used by the "all cards" activation flow to list cards the member can activate Credit Flex on (excludes Team cards and already-activated cards).

  4. GET /credit-flex-cards/{cardId} used by the single-card details screen and the per-card activation flow.

  5. POST /credit-flex-cards/{cardId} - first-time activation for a card

  6. PATCH /credit-flex-cards/{cardId} - change toggle / min on an already-activated card.

Loan creation:

  1. credit_flex_loan_application audit table tracking every settlement-driven loan application Cards initiates with tide-credit-svc, plus the lifecycle updates received via public events.

  2. TransactionClearedEventProcessor - updated to publish an EvaluateCreditFlexCommand to an SQS queue consumed by expense-card-svc for any cleared card transaction; never calls Credit synchronously, never blocks the settlement processing path.

  3. EvaluateCreditFlexCommandProcessor - runs the auto-flex evaluation, persists the audit row, and calls tide-credit-svc POST /api/v4/tide-credit/applications when all checks pass.

  4. CreditFlexEventProcessor - consumes events from tide-credit-svc (LoanApprovedEventV1, LoanRejectedEventV1) and updates the audit row.

  5. BOT: GET /agent-api/v4/credit-flex-cards/{cardId} to get full information (status, min transaction amount, loan applications) about card & credit flex

Which service should own the logic

The Credit Flex feature will be implemented in the new expense-card-svc, which will own the credit_flex_card and credit_flex_loan_application tables, the EvaluateCreditFlexCommandProcessor, and the CreditFlexEventProcessor consuming events from tide-credit-svc.

PROS:

  • Clear bounded context: Credit Flex (and future expense-card capabilities such as marketing campaigns and cashback hooks) lives separately from core card lifecycle, avoiding bloat in card-svc.

  • Independent scaling and deployment: settlement-time evaluation throughput and loan-application workflows can be tuned without affecting card issuance/transactions paths.

  • Foundation for future expense-card features: establishes the home for auxiliary card-related functionality (marketing campaigns, cashback hooks, etc.) that does not belong in card-svc.

  • No cross-service URL splits: /credit-flex-cards/* is owned end-to-end by expense-card-svc; /cards/* remains fully owned by card-svc.

CONS:

  • Cross-service data reads: expense-card-svc will need to read card data (type, status, ownership) from card-svc rather than from a local DB.

  • Operational surface area: one more service to monitor, alert on, and own.

6.2 Dashboard 1 — “Credit Flex on Cards — Executive”

Goal: Adoption, utilisation, and whether the feature moves card spend and credit volume in the intended direction.

  • Eligible base: # members credit-eligible for Credit Flex (latest snapshot); trend over time.

  • Activation (member): % of eligible members with ≥1 card activated (credit_flex_card.activated_on not null).

  • Activation (card): # cards activated; avg cards activated per activated member; split physical vs virtual vs ODC.

  • Enabled (live): # cards with enabled=true; % of activated cards currently ON.

  • First value: % of members who enable ≥1 card and receive ≥1 successful auto-flex loan within 7d / 30d of first enable.

  • Auto-flex volume: # loans created from card settlement (approved); total £ flexed; avg £ per loan; loans per active card per month.

  • Credit utilisation: % of remaining credit line consumed by card auto-flex (member/card level aggregates); distribution of threshold settings (£100–£1000).

  • Card spend impact (primary PRD KPI): For members with ≥1 card CF-enabled vs matched control (or pre/post cohort):

    • Δ cleared card txn count and volume (7d / 30d / 90d windows)

    • Δ spend on txns ≥ £100 (and ≥ member’s configured threshold)

    • Δ share of card spend that is “large” (>£100)

  • Secondary: virtual card issuance and usage among CF adopters vs non-adopters.

  • Quality: support ticket volume tagged Credit Flex + Cards; complaint rate per 1k activated members.

6.3 Dashboard 2 — “Credit Flex on Cards — Funnel & Drivers”

Goal: Where users enter, where they drop off, and what drives activation.

Entry points to attribute (every view/click must carry entryPoint): cards_home_banner, card_settings, all_cards_activation, push_eligibility_paused, push_eligibility_restored, txn_detail_not_flexed, credit_dashboard_deeplink.

Member-level funnel (first time on any card):

  1. Eligible member sees Credit Flex entry (banner or card settings)

  2. Explainer / “Get Credit Flex” viewed

  3. Review offer screen viewed

  4. T&Cs / PG accepted (tcVersion)

  5. First card POST activation success

  6. Toggle turned ON + threshold set

  7. First successful auto-flex loan

Per-card funnel (subsequent cards): card settings viewed → POST activate → toggle ON → first flex on that card.

  • Conversion rates between steps; median time between steps.

  • Drop-off: “Not now” on offer screen; activate but never enable toggle; enable but no qualifying txn in 30d.

  • Breakdowns: entry point, card type, threshold bucket, plan tier (if available).

6.4 Dashboard 3 — “Credit Flex on Cards — Auto-flex engine”

Goal: Operational and product visibility into settlement-time behaviour (from credit_flex_loan_application + evaluation logs).

  • Evaluations: # cleared card txns evaluated per day; evaluation rate vs all cleared txns on CF-enabled cards.

  • Outcomes (volume & %):

    • Loan approved (flexed)

    • Rejected by Credit (failure_reason from credit)

    • Skipped — toggle OFF

    • Skipped — member not eligible

    • Skipped — below threshold

    • Skipped — credit line cap reached

    • Skipped — MCC / txn type ineligible (e.g. ATM)

    • Failed — technical / timeout (PRD: remains normal card txn)

  • Flex rate: approved loans / qualifying opportunities (txns ≥ threshold on enabled+eligible cards).

  • Latency: p50/p95 time from settlement to loan approved / failed.

  • Threshold sensitivity: flex rate and £ volume by configured min amount bucket.

  • Re-toggle behaviour: # members who turn OFF then ON (T&C re-shown per PRD) — track repeat acceptance.

6.5 Dashboard 4 — “Credit Flex on Cards — Eligibility & lifecycle”

Goal: Understand eligibility churn, pause/resume, and dormant configurations.

  • Eligibility transitions: # members eligible → not eligible → eligible per credit check cycle; % with ≥1 card previously ON when paused.

  • Paused state: # cards with stored config but auto-flex paused; avg duration of pause.

  • Re-enable: After eligibility returns — auto re-enable vs manual (per PRD recommendation: auto for cards that were ON); notification open rate.

  • Churn / dormancy:

    • Activated but toggle never ON (7d)

    • ON but no flex in 30d (enabled but unused)

    • ON → OFF (user disabled) without deleting activation

  • Config changes: # threshold changes per card per month; distribution of edits.

6.6 Dashboard 5 — “Credit Flex on Cards — Before vs after (launch readout)”

Goal: Answer “are people spending more now?” with a defensible methodology.

  • Pre-period: 90 days before member’s first enabled=true (or first activation — AE to confirm anchor).

  • Post-period: 30 / 90 days after anchor.

  • Metrics per member: cleared card txn count, volume, count ≥£100, interchange if available.

  • Comparison modes (implement at least one):

    • Within-member pre/post (primary)

    • Matched control: eligible, not activated, similar pre-period spend

    • Diff-in-diff vs eligible non-adopters (launch window)

  • Guardrails: exclude Team cards; flag seasonality; segment by card type and industry if available.
