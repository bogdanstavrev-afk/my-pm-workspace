# Outcome Framing Skill

## Purpose
Transform vague ideas into measurable outcomes.

## Core Goal
Move teams from shipping features to achieving measurable business outcomes.

## Inputs
- Business problem
- User pain
- Strategic goals
- Operational inefficiencies
- Market opportunity

## Outputs
- Current state
- Root problem
- Desired outcomes
- KPIs
- Success metrics
- MVP slices
- AI opportunities
- Human-in-the-loop boundaries

## Core Questions
- What outcome are we trying to create?
- Why does this problem exist?
- What operational burden exists today?
- What measurable improvement matters?
- What should AI assist vs automate?

## Success Criteria
- Clear measurable goals
- Defined target state
- Defined user value
- Defined business impact
- Clear prioritization
