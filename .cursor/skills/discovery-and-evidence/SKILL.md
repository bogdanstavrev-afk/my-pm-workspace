# Discovery & Evidence Skill

## Purpose
Use AI to synthesize evidence, signals, and market intelligence.

## Core Goal
Replace opinion-driven discovery with evidence-driven discovery.

## Inputs
- User feedback
- Support tickets
- Market signals
- Competitor analysis
- Behavioral analytics
- Research docs
- Internal operational pain points

## Outputs
- User pain clusters
- Market opportunities
- Root cause insights
- Behavioral patterns
- Prioritized opportunity areas
- Evidence-backed recommendations

## AI Responsibilities
- Cluster themes
- Analyze sentiment
- Detect repeated friction
- Surface unmet needs
- Summarize research
- Detect anomalies
- Compare competitors
